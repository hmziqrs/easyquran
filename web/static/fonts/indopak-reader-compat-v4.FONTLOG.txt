Versions 2 and 3 were derived from Noto Naskh Arabic 2.021 (see their own FONTLOG files).

Why the new base: IndoPak mushafs, and Quran.com's IndoPak reader, use South Asian letterforms
(lam, qaf, kaf, yeh, heh) and heavier strokes. Lateef draws that tradition under OFL; Noto
Naskh is a Middle-Eastern design. No glyph, outline or layout table from any restricted font
(including Quran.com's AlQuran IndoPak by QuranWBW) is used or traced.

Modifications:
- Retain the full original repertoire, GSUB/GPOS/GDEF and all standard Unicode mappings.
- Add nine BMP private mappings onto Lateef's own dedicated Quranic glyphs.
- Reuse genuine Lateef subscript alef (U+0656) and high ain (U+08D6) mark glyphs.
- Compose zero-advance E004 inverted damma from Lateef U+0657, translated (239,-353),
  for its standalone L bidi run in the sole immutable corpus occurrence, 17:7.
- Compose five spacing pause glyphs (zain, sad, qaf, qif, waqfa) from Lateef U+0617,
  U+08D5, U+08D7, U+08DE, U+08DF with 123-unit side bearings and GDEF base class; qif and
  waqfa, drawn at baseline height in Lateef, are lifted to the shared pause lane (y >= 1000).
- Compose E021 optional-ayah from Lateef U+08E2 at half scale, translated (-800,1390), in
  separate horizontal and vertical ink lanes from following pause signs; GDEF mark class.
- Map the U+FE8E alef-final presentation form to Lateef's own final alef glyph.
- Redraw the 1-, 2- and 3-digit end-of-ayah enclosures as plain rings (Lateef's "Simplified A"
  geometry, 110-unit stroke); digit composition and placement remain Lateef's.
- Set usWeightClass 400 so the face is served as the regular weight.
- Rename family, PostScript, full, unique, typographic, WWS and description records.
- Package genuine TrueType TTF and WOFF2 without subsetting layout dependencies.

Browser integration (web/src/routes/(application)/_reader/IndoPakAyah.svelte) serves the font
with size-adjust 125%, lays every word out as one unbreakable box as Quran.com does, keeps
pause signs with their word, stacks trailing pause/ruku signs above the ring, and keeps the
final Arabic word with its number. Exact original text and DOM order remain. Cmap aliases do
not change Unicode properties. The E004 correction is corpus/renderer scoped.

No Quran data modified, normalized or included in build. Printed-edition provenance and
qualified recitation review remain separate items.
