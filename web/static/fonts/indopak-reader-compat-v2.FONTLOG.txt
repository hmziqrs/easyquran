Modifications:
- Instantiate weight 400; retain the full original repertoire and layout.
- Add nine BMP private mappings while preserving all standard Unicode mappings.
- Reuse genuine Noto subscript alef and high ain mark glyphs.
- Compose zero-advance E004 inverted damma from Noto U+0657, translated (122,-377),
  for its standalone L bidi run in the sole immutable corpus occurrence, 17:7.
- Compose five spacing pause glyphs from their dedicated Noto annotation glyphs;
  preserve outline height, assign nonzero advances and GDEF base class.
- Compose E021 optional-ayah 5 from dedicated Noto U+08E2 at half scale, translated
  (-250,820). Separate its ink from following pause signs. Remove v1's incorrect
  shared ruku attachment anchor. Both private marks retain GDEF mark class.
- Add outline-free U+2003 em space with one-em advance.
- Rename family, PostScript, full, unique, typographic and WWS name records.
- Package genuine TrueType TTF and WOFF2 without subsetting layout dependencies.

Browser integration places trailing zain/ruku signs above the ayah ornament and
keeps the final word with its number. Exact original text and DOM order remain.
Cmap aliases do not change Unicode properties; tests include actual browser bidi
itemization, rather than treating forced whole-run HarfBuzz as position approval.
The E004 correction is corpus/renderer scoped, not a general Unicode-property fix.

No restricted font assets used. Reference inspection establishes identities and
sampled placement only. No Quran data modified, normalized or included in build.
Printed-edition provenance and qualified recitation review remain separate items.
