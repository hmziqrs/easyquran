1. **Ban key / retention (Option B):** ban on `device_record_id`; a live ban pins the record; the
   record/links live 30 d of inactivity otherwise; the ban row lives 12 mo; the 1-year re-mint rotates
   `token_hash` in place; never revoke on ban. One sentence, in the schema comment and the SOP.
2. **Token storage:** `ruxlog.device` HttpOnly, explicit `Path=/api`; "no cookie on reader paths"; the
   cache-restoration fix is E6 P0.1; the token is Phase 4, after Gate B.
3. **Enforcement truth:** no ban can be issued today (zero writers); no notice can be proven delivered
   (the router discards suppression); the gates are not enforced; the ingress identity is forgeable.
   Phase 0 fixes the last two; Phase 1 fixes the first two — in that order.
