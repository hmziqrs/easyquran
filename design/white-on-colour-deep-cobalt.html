Why it is built this way

Start with the text done right, then grow around it.

Most Qur’an apps try to do everything at once. easyquran works the other way around — a page that opens fast, shows the Uthmani script clearly, and then gets out of the way. The Arabic text and every translation come from Tanzil.net, used unchanged under their terms.

Why we built it
1 Open it No install, no account, no setup screen.
2 Read it Uthmani script, scaled to whatever your eyes want.
3 Close it Free, no ads, nothing tracking what you read.
